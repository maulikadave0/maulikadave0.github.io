This article revieews a book on Hypergraph computations [1]. The book prepares the reader to use hypergraphs for designing data structures in software appications. Initial chapters describe features of hypergraphs. Later chapters describe how to use hypergraphs in real life applications. The features include mathematical foundation, typical computational tasks, guidence for storing large hypergraphs, and neural networks for deep learning on hypergraphs. The applications are in the fields of social media, medicine, biology, and computer vision. Chapters 1 and 13 are for general readers. Chapter 2 and 4 are for designers. Chaper 3, 5, and 8 are for computation oriened readers. Remaining chapters are for AI and applications oriened readers.

Reeferences

[1] Qionghai Dai, Yue Gao, "Hypergraph Computation", Springer, https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-99-0185-2
