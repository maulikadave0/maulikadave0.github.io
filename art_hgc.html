This article revieews a book on Hypergraph computations [1]. The book prepares the reader to use hypergraphs for designing data structures in software appications. Initial chapters describe features of hypergraphs. Later chapters describe how to use hypergraphs in real life applications. The features include mathematical foundation, typical computational tasks, guidence for storing large hypergraphs, and neural networks for deep learning on hypergraphs. The applications are in the fields of social media, medicine, biology, and computer vision. Chapters 1 and 13 are for general readers. Chapter 2 and 4 are for designers. Chaper 3, 5, and 8 are for computation oriened readers. Remaining chapters are for AI and applications oriented readers. As per authors, the book can be used at undergraduate, graduate, and research levels.

Some Points from the book

  • Compared with the simple graph which can only model the pairwise relationship between two subjects, hypergraph is a flexible and representative model to formulate high-order correlations. (Chapter 1 in [1])
  • The traditional hypergraph structure creates associations among vertices, with a single hyperedge connecting multiple vertices that have associations. (Chapter 2 in [1])
  • Currently, there are a number of methods for converting a hypergraph to a simple graph. The common ones are clique expansion, star expansion, and line expansion. (Chapter 2 in [1])
  • The implicit hypergraph modeling strategy aims to generate the hypergraph structure using vertex representations based on their distances or similarities, in which the correlations are not directly provided. The explicit hypergraph modeling strategy targets at the data with explicit high-order correlation information, such as attributes and pairwise connections. (Chapter 4 in [1]).
  • To help solve the large scale problem, the book introduces two types of hypergraph computational methods to handle large scale data in this chapter, namely the factorization-based hypergraph reduction method and hierarchical hypergraph computation method. (Chapter 8 in [1])
  • The hypergraphs can be constructed by modeling the high order relationship among inter- or intra-visual samples, and then computer vision tasks can be solved by hypergraph computation procedures. (Chapter 11 in [1])
  • The DeepHypergraph library bridges the hypergraph theory and hypergraph applications. (Chapter 12 in [1]).
